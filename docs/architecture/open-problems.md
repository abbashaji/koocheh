# Open problems

Stated, not solved. Items 1 to 7 come from spec v1.0; items 8 to 10 were raised during planning and are not in spec v1.0.

1. **Legal and personal risk.** The decision belongs to local advisors and to users. The software does not settle it.
2. **Entrapment and infiltration without a central moderator.** Mutual tiers, vouching and local blocks narrow what a hostile participant gets, and do not stop entrapment or infiltration.
3. **Gossip density and latency.** Untested in Tehran.
4. **Battery cost of continuous BLE scanning** on mid-range phones.
5. **PSI performance and correctness** on low-end devices.
6. **Transfer of research.** Most of the supporting research uses Western student samples.
7. **Compelled unlock.** No technical feature defeats a phone that is opened while unlocked.
8. **Familiar-stranger linking (spec 5.5) is unspecified.** Ephemeral identifiers rotate every 10 to 15 minutes, so repeat encounters cannot be linked without some stable or semi-stable handle, which conflicts with unlinkability.
9. **Tier 0 shows a shared-interest count before names.** This needs a cardinality-first PSI variant. Whether a maintained mobile library offers one is unknown.
10. **Offline single-use invite enforcement.** A photographed QR code can be replayed unless the token is bound to the new install's key.
