# Pilot Risk Briefing

This is the briefing every person must read and understand before joining the pilot. Joining is voluntary and you can stop at any time. Please read all of it. If something is unclear, ask before you decide.

## What this app is

It is a pilot of an app that lets nearby people connect and message without going through a company server. It is experimental software. It has not been proven safe.

## What we have and have not done

- An independent security check and a tracking test are required before the pilot. This briefing should only be used once the gate checklist shows they were done and their results are recorded.
- Even a clean result means only that the testers found no serious problems in what they tried. It does not mean the app is safe.

## Risks you need to know

### The app cannot remove legal or social consequences
Using the app, or being found with it, may have legal or social consequences for you. The app cannot change the law, stop someone from reporting you, or undo what other people think or do. You are responsible for judging the risk where you live.

### Entrapment and infiltration are unsolved
Someone may join pretending to be a normal user in order to learn who you are, collect messages, or lure you into harm. The app cannot reliably tell who a person really is. Do not assume that a person is safe because they are in the app or came through an invite.

### Compelled unlock is not defended against
If someone forces you to unlock your phone or hand over your PIN, by threat, pressure, or legal order, the app does not protect you. The decoy and wipe features may help in some situations but are not a guarantee. They can fail, and using or triggering them may itself draw suspicion.

### Tracking and detection may still be possible
Tests can only look for the problems we thought of. Someone with better equipment or methods might still detect the app or follow your phone through Bluetooth signals.

### Your phone and what you share
- The app cannot protect you if your phone has spyware, if someone else uses your phone, or if another person screenshots or shares your messages.
- Anything you choose to disclose to another person is out of the app's control once they have it.
- Notifications, app icons, and permissions on your phone may show that the app exists.

### Bugs and data loss
The software may crash, lose messages, or expose information through mistakes we have not found. A wipe cannot be undone.

## What we ask of you

- Think about your own situation before joining. If the risk is too high for you, do not join.
- Do not rely on this app for anything where a failure would put you in danger.
- Report any problem, suspicious behaviour, or harm to the team through the contact channel you were given. Reporting is never penalized.

## Your choices

- You can say no. You can leave at any time, uninstall, and wipe your data.
- Leaving does not undo anything that already happened or anything other people kept.
